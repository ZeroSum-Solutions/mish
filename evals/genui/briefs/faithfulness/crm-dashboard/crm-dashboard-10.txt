Amaranth Development Office runs major-gift fundraising for a regional arts museum from an office at 900 Fine Arts Way in St. Louis, Missouri, with a team of four gift officers. Every morning the director wants four numbers first: dollars raised this month, major-gift conversations currently open, average gift size, and year-over-year membership renewal rate. Below that sits a pipeline table listing one row per prospect — the donor's last name, the gift officer who owns the relationship, the stage from identified through cultivating, solicited, and closed, the ask amount, and a plain-language next step. An activity feed shows short lines for layout purposes only, such as 'Officer H. Grantham logged a stewardship visit with donor R. Feldstein, moved to solicited, 30 minutes ago.' One contact card should surface the largest open ask currently being cultivated, and a chart shows dollars raised per week over the last eight weeks. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at development@amaranthmuseum.org or (314) 555-0149. Amaranth Development Office is part of a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Missouri. Dashboard filters run by officer and by stage.
