Brackenfield Recruiting runs a six-recruiter desk placing mid-level engineering talent for client companies from an office at 233 South Wacker Drive, Suite 1400, in Chicago, Illinois. Every morning the desk lead wants four numbers first: open searches this week, candidates submitted, placements made this month, and placement fee revenue booked this month. Below that sits a pipeline table listing the eight searches closest to a placement, each with the client company, the recruiter who owns it, the stage from sourcing to submitted to interviewing to offer, the fee value, and the next scheduled step. An activity feed shows short lines for layout purposes only, such as 'Recruiter A. Voss submitted a candidate to Harrow Manufacturing, moved to interviewing, 25 minutes ago.' One contact card highlights the client search closest to an offer, and a chart shows candidates submitted per week over the last eight weeks. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., at desk@brackenfieldrecruiting.com or (312) 555-0129. Brackenfield Recruiting is a registered staffing agency in the State of Illinois.
