Cobalt Fieldnotes is an independent online magazine covering small-scale manufacturing and the people who run machine shops, workshops, and fabrication studios across the country, published from an editorial office at 501 East Sixth Street in Austin, Texas. A first-time reader usually arrives from a shared link and reads one story before deciding whether to come back, so the homepage needs a headline-driven layout surfacing the newest long-form profiles alongside a handful of evergreen how-we-built-it guides. Each article page should show the author's byline and a short bio, three related stories at the bottom, and an unobtrusive subscribe prompt placed after the second paragraph rather than as a pop-up. The magazine publishes two new features a week and sends a Friday newsletter recapping everything published that week, currently reaching around eighteen thousand subscribers. The editorial office is staffed Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at editors@cobaltfieldnotes.com or (512) 555-0138. Cobalt Fieldnotes is published by a registered Texas media LLC. The primary call to action across the site is subscribing to the free weekly newsletter.
