Thornfield Insurance Adjusters handles property-damage claims for a regional insurer from an office at 45 Court Street in Hartford, Connecticut, and its team of ten field adjusters shares one internal tool to move every claim from filed to settled. The main screen lists open claims showing the claim number, the policyholder's last name, the damage type, current status, and the adjuster assigned — a sample row shown only to illustrate layout might read 'CLM-30841, Ferris, roof damage, inspection scheduled, assigned to T. Novak.' A claim's detail page holds the policy details, inspection photos, the repair estimate, and a running note thread between the adjuster and the policyholder. Side navigation separates the claim queue, the adjuster roster, and a weekly cycle-time report. Two settings the team relies on: the dollar threshold above which a claim requires a supervisor's review before settlement, and the number of days a claim can go untouched before it is flagged as overdue. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., at claims@thornfieldadjusters.com or (860) 555-0163. Thornfield Insurance Adjusters is licensed to adjust claims in the State of Connecticut.
