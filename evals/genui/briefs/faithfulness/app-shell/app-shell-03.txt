Foxhollow Veterinary Group runs three clinic locations around Madison, Wisconsin from an administrative office at 620 University Avenue, and its front-desk staff of nine share one internal scheduling and records tool across all three sites. The main screen is a day view of appointments per location showing the pet's name, owner, the visit reason, assigned veterinarian, and status — a sample line for layout purposes only might read 'Bishop (canine), 10:30 a.m., annual wellness, Dr. Alvarez, checked in.' Opening an appointment shows the pet's chart summary, vaccination history, any standing alerts like allergies, and a field to log the visit outcome and next recommended date. Side navigation separates the daily schedule, patient records, inventory, and billing. Two settings the practice depends on: which staff can override a fully booked slot for an urgent case, and the lead time required before a client can cancel without a fee. The clinics are open Monday through Saturday, 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., with scheduling support at (608) 555-0148 or scheduling@foxhollowvet.com. Foxhollow Veterinary Group is licensed to practice veterinary medicine in the State of Wisconsin.
