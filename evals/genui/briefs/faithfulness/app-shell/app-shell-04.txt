Ledger Peak Bookkeeping serves ninety small-business clients from an office at 1500 Market Street, Suite 810, in Denver, Colorado, and its team of seven bookkeepers shares one internal tool to track monthly close work across every client. The main screen lists open client tasks showing the client name, the task type such as reconciliation or payroll review, the assigned bookkeeper, status, and due date — a sample row shown only to illustrate layout might read 'Harlow Design Co, bank reconciliation, assigned to R. Chen, in review, due the 5th.' A task's detail view holds the client's chart of accounts snapshot, any flagged discrepancies, notes exchanged with the client, and a checklist of the close steps completed. Side navigation separates clients, the monthly close queue, and a reports section for firm-wide status. Two settings the firm relies on: which bookkeepers can mark a client task as final without a second review, and how many days before month-end close reminders start going out. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., at support@ledgerpeakbooks.com or (303) 555-0159. Ledger Peak Bookkeeping is a registered Colorado business.
