Havenwell Legal Aid provides free civil legal help to low-income residents of three counties from an intake office at 77 State Street in Albany, New York, where four intake coordinators share one internal tool to screen and route new cases. The main screen lists open intake requests showing the applicant's name, the case category such as housing or family law, income-eligibility status, assigned attorney, and current stage — a sample row for layout only might read 'R. Nguyen, housing eviction defense, eligible, assigned to Atty. Park, awaiting documents.' Opening a case shows the applicant's full intake questionnaire, uploaded documents, eligibility calculation, and case notes visible only to staff assigned to that matter. Side navigation separates intake, active cases, the attorney roster, and a monthly reporting view required by the organization's funders. Two settings that matter to this team: the income threshold that determines eligibility for each case type, and how many days an unassigned case can sit before it is escalated to the intake supervisor. The intake line is staffed Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., at intake@havenwelllegalaid.org or (518) 555-0136. Havenwell Legal Aid is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit in New York.
