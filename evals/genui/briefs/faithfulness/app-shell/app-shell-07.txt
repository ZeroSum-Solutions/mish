Craneridge Fleet Services manages preventive maintenance for a regional delivery company's 140-vehicle fleet from a shop at 900 Depot Street in Louisville, Kentucky, and its five technicians share one internal tool to track every vehicle's service state. The main screen lists vehicles due or overdue for service, showing the vehicle ID, current mileage, the service type due, assigned bay, and status — a sample row shown only to illustrate layout might read 'Van 118, 42,300 miles, oil and brake inspection, Bay 2, in progress.' A vehicle's detail page holds its full service history, open recall notices, the technician's current notes, and photos of any flagged damage. Side navigation separates the service queue, the vehicle roster, parts inventory, and a monthly compliance report. Two settings the shop relies on: the mileage interval that triggers each service type, and which technicians are authorized to sign off a vehicle as road-ready after a safety inspection. The shop is staffed Monday through Saturday, 6 a.m. to 4 p.m., at fleet@craneridgeservices.com or (502) 555-0144. Craneridge Fleet Services is a licensed commercial vehicle repair facility in Kentucky.
