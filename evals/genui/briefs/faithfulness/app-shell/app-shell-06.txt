Stillwater Catering Co caters weddings and corporate events across the Minneapolis-St. Paul area from a commissary kitchen at 3300 Hiawatha Avenue in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and its team of eight event coordinators shares one internal tool to run every booked event from deposit to breakdown. The main screen lists upcoming events showing the client name, event date, guest count, assigned lead coordinator, and prep status — a sample row shown only to illustrate the layout might read 'Alden-Ferris wedding, June 14, 180 guests, lead K. Sato, tasting complete.' An event's detail page holds the signed menu, dietary-restriction notes, the staffing plan, the equipment checklist, and a day-of timeline shared with the venue. Side navigation separates the event calendar, menus, staffing, and vendor contacts. Two settings the company relies on: how many days before an event the final guest count locks, and which coordinators are authorized to approve a menu change inside two weeks of the date. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at events@stillwatercatering.com or (612) 555-0127. Stillwater Catering Co is a licensed food-service caterer in the State of Minnesota.
