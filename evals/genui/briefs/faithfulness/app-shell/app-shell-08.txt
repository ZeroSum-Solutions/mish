Oakmere Events operates a single event venue at 210 Riverside Drive in Savannah, Georgia, and its four-person booking team shares one internal tool to manage every inquiry from first contact through the signed contract. The main screen lists open inquiries showing the prospective client's name, requested event date, guest count, assigned coordinator, and stage — a sample row shown only to illustrate the layout might read 'Whitfield anniversary, October 4, 90 guests, coordinator J. Byrne, contract sent.' Opening an inquiry shows the client's full requirements, any date holds on the calendar, the proposal sent with pricing, and a message log with the client. Side navigation separates the inquiry queue, the venue calendar, vendor partners, and a monthly booked-revenue report. Two settings the team relies on: how many days a tentative date hold is kept before it releases automatically, and which coordinators can approve a custom pricing exception. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., at events@oakmerevenue.com or (912) 555-0151. Oakmere Events holds a valid Georgia event venue and alcohol service license.
