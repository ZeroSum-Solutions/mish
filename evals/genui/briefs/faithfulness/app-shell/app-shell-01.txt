Marrowgate Property Group manages 410 rental units across the Tampa Bay area from its office at 2100 Bayshore Boulevard in Tampa, Florida, and five coordinators share one internal tool to run repair requests from report to close. The main screen is a list of open jobs showing the property address, the reported issue, the assigned contractor, current status, and when it last changed — for example, a row might read '118 Palm Court, leaking faucet, assigned to Ruiz Plumbing, in progress, updated 2 hours ago,' which is a sample row for layout only. Opening a job shows the tenant's original report with any photos, the contractor's quote, the scheduled date, and a sign-off field once the tenant confirms the fix. Side navigation separates jobs, properties, contractors, and a weekly reports section. Two settings matter to this team: which contractors are approved for after-hours emergency call-outs, and the hours during which tenants may be contacted directly. The office operates Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., with an after-hours line at (813) 555-0162 or ops@marrowgatepm.com. Marrowgate Property Group is a licensed property manager in the State of Florida.
