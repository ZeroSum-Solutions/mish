Basincreek Logistics runs the returns desk for three retail partners from a distribution center at 4500 Freight Yard Road in Memphis, Tennessee, where six processors handle pallets of returned merchandise every day. The working list shows the order number, the retail partner, the reason code such as damaged or wrong item, the current status such as received, inspected, or restocked, and the assigned processor — a sample row for the design might read 'ORD-88214, Northgate Retail, wrong item, inspected, assigned to D. Ochoa,' shown purely to illustrate the layout. A case's detail page should hold photos of the item's condition, the original order details, the restock-or-write-off decision, and any message thread with the retail partner about a disputed return. Two settings the team relies on: the dollar threshold above which a case needs a supervisor's sign-off, and how many days a case can sit untouched before it is flagged as stale. The returns desk is staffed Monday through Saturday, 6 a.m. to 6 p.m., reachable at returns@basincreeklogistics.com or (901) 555-0173. Basincreek Logistics operates as a licensed freight and warehousing company registered in Tennessee.
