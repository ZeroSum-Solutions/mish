Millbrook Home Health coordinates in-home nursing visits for elderly patients across two counties from an office at 88 Concord Street in Manchester, New Hampshire, and its six schedulers share one internal tool to assign and track every visit. The main screen lists today's visits showing the patient's name, assigned nurse, visit type such as wound care or medication management, scheduled time, and status — a sample row shown only to illustrate layout might read 'E. Whitcombe, nurse R. Bellamy, wound care, 11:00 a.m., completed.' A visit's detail page holds the patient's care plan, the nurse's visit notes, any flagged concerns for the supervising physician, and a signature confirming the visit occurred. Side navigation separates today's schedule, the patient roster, the nurse roster, and a compliance report required by the state. Two settings the office relies on: how far in advance a visit must be confirmed before it is flagged as at-risk, and which schedulers can reassign a nurse's caseload. The office is staffed Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., at scheduling@millbrookhomehealth.com or (603) 555-0132. Millbrook Home Health is licensed as a home health agency by the State of New Hampshire.
