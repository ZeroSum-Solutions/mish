Tidewater Data Cohort is a twelve-week evening course in Norfolk, Virginia, that teaches working adults to analyse data with spreadsheets, SQL and Python, taught in person at 400 Granby Street, Floor 2. A visitor here is deciding whether the course will get them a first analyst role, so open with the outcome: every graduate finishes with three portfolio projects built on public Hampton Roads data. The curriculum runs in three four-week modules: spreadsheets and data cleaning, SQL and databases, then Python and a capstone presented to local employers. Two instructors teach every cohort: Priya Raman, a former analyst at a regional hospital system, and Luis Ortega, a data engineer at a shipping logistics firm. Classes meet Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 6 to 9 p.m., and the next cohort starts Tuesday, January 12, 2027, with sixteen seats. Tuition is four thousand eight hundred dollars, payable in four monthly instalments. One graduate, Dana Whitfield of the spring 2026 cohort, says: "The capstone was the project I talked about in every interview." Answer common questions about laptops (bring your own) and missed classes (sessions are recorded). Admissions is reachable at enroll@tidewaterdata.org or (757) 555-0118. The primary call to action is enrolling in the next cohort.
