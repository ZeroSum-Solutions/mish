Briarcross Marketplace connects homeowners with vetted independent contractors — electricians, plumbers, and handypeople — across the greater Denver metro area, operating from an office at 1450 Larimer Street in Denver, Colorado. A homeowner comes to this site with an urgent repair and needs to search by trade and zip code, filter results by rating and response time, and compare at least three profiles before requesting a quote. Every contractor profile should show verified license numbers, insurance status, a portfolio of past jobs with photos, and unedited homeowner reviews, because trust in a stranger doing work in someone's house is the whole product. Explain the safeguard that makes this different from an open listing board: Briarcross escrows payment until the homeowner confirms the job is done, and every contractor is background-checked before joining. Support is available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and weekends 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at support@briarcrossmarketplace.com or (720) 555-0184. Briarcross Marketplace is a registered business operating in the State of Colorado. Close with a request-a-quote call to action on every contractor profile.
