Cascade Freight Claims processes cargo damage claims for a regional trucking carrier headquartered at 900 Airport Way South in Seattle, Washington, handling roughly ninety new claims a month from shippers and receivers. The three-person claims team needs an internal tool built around one record type: a claim, tied to a shipment, with a status that moves from filed to under review to settled or denied. The working list should show the claim number, the shipper's name, the dollar amount claimed, the current status, and the adjuster assigned, sorted so anything untouched for more than five days surfaces first. A claim's detail page needs the original bill of lading, photos of the damaged freight, the adjuster's notes, and a running message thread with the shipper so nothing gets lost between calls. Two settings the team cares about: the dollar threshold above which a claim needs a supervisor's sign-off, and how many days a claim can sit before it's flagged as overdue. The claims office is staffed Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific, at claims@cascadefreight.com or (206) 555-0158. Cascade Freight Claims operates under its parent carrier's motor carrier authority, licensed in Washington State.
