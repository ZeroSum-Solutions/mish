Pinewell Property Management runs the repair coordination desk for 260 rental units across greater Charlotte, North Carolina, from its office at 5601 Executive Center Drive. Four coordinators work the same internal tool all day to take tenant repair requests, assign approved contractors, and close jobs once work is verified. The main screen is a list of open jobs showing the property address, the reported issue, the assigned contractor, current status, and when it last changed, so a coordinator can scan for anything stalled. Opening a job shows the tenant's original report with any photos, the contractor's quote, the scheduled date, and a sign-off field once the tenant confirms the fix. Side navigation should separate jobs, properties, contractors, and a reports section coordinators check weekly. Two settings matter to this team: which contractors are approved for after-hours emergency call-outs, and the hours during which tenants may be contacted directly. The office operates Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., with an after-hours line at (704) 555-0147 or ops@pinewellpm.com. Pinewell Property Management is a licensed property manager in North Carolina. The tool's core action is moving a job from open to closed with a verified sign-off.
