Stonebridge Culinary School trains career-changers and working cooks in a nine-month professional certificate program at its teaching kitchen at 615 Bannock Street in Boise, Idaho. Prospective students land on this site while comparing culinary schools and need to see the outcome first: graduates line-cook or sous-chef within four months of finishing, at an average starting wage the school tracks and publishes every year. Walk visitors through the curriculum term by term — knife skills and stocks, butchery and baking, then a six-week externship at a partner restaurant — and introduce the four chef-instructors with their restaurant backgrounds. Feature student work through photos of plated dishes from the final capstone, and include real testimonials naming the restaurants graduates now work at. Tuition is fourteen thousand dollars with a payment plan and two need-based scholarships awarded per cohort. Admissions is open Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., reachable at admissions@stonebridgeculinary.edu or (208) 555-0139. Stonebridge Culinary School is licensed as a postsecondary institution by the Idaho State Board of Education. Close with an apply-now call to action and the next cohort start date.
