The Ridgeline Ledger is an independent local newsroom and weekly newsletter covering city and county government in Asheville, North Carolina, published from an office at 14 Wall Street, Suite 3. Readers arrive from a shared link or a search and need a headline-driven homepage: this week's lead story is "The water board's $40 million pipe plan, line by line" by Marisol Keane, the paper's county reporter. Below it, list the latest stories with their publish dates: "Why the bus route map changed again" (September 18), "Inside the county's first open budget hearing" (September 11), and "Three candidates, one school board seat" (September 4). Topics are City Hall, County, Schools, Transit and Housing. Two running series anchor the site: Meeting Notes, a plain-language recap of every council meeting, and Follow the Money, on how public dollars are spent. The editor is Aaron Pike, formerly of a regional daily. The free newsletter arrives every Friday morning. Readers can stay free or become a member for eight dollars a month, which unlocks the full archive and a monthly call with the reporters. Reach the newsroom at tips@ridgelineledger.org or (828) 555-0135. The Ridgeline Ledger is a registered North Carolina nonprofit. The primary call to action is subscribing to the newsletter.
