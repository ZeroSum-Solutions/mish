Coppervale Outfitters sells handmade leather goods — belts, wallets, and camp bags — from a workshop and retail storefront at 340 Elm Street in Missoula, Montana, alongside its online store. The company was founded by two saddle-makers and now ships nationwide, so the site needs a product grid that lets a shopper browse by category, see full-grain leather color options, and read the story behind each stitching pattern before adding to cart. Product pages should show close-up photography, a size and care guide, and real customer reviews, since buyers hesitate over durability and fit for goods this price point. Reassure shoppers with a lifetime restitching guarantee, free exchanges within sixty days, and flat five-dollar shipping on orders over seventy-five dollars. The storefront is open Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and the online store never closes. Reach the team at orders@coppervaleoutfitters.com or (406) 555-0142. Coppervale Outfitters is a registered retailer and collects Montana state sales tax as required by law. Every product page should end with a clear add-to-cart action and a note on current processing time.
