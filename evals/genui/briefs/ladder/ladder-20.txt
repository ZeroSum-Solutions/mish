Brightline Advocacy Network campaigns for expanded public transit funding across three counties from a field office at 501 Grand Avenue in Des Moines, Iowa, coordinating volunteers and organizing testimony before the state legislature. A visitor to this site usually already agrees with the goal in general terms and needs a reason to act today, so open with the specific issue on the table this session — a transit funding bill up for a committee vote — and the Network's position in plain language. Back the position with evidence: ridership data, a summary of the economic case, and short video testimony from riders who depend on the routes at risk. Show the path from agreement to action clearly: sign a petition, show up to one of three public hearings this month, or contact an assigned state representative through a pre-filled form. An events section lists upcoming hearings and volunteer canvassing shifts with sign-up links. The field office is staffed Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., at organize@brightlineadvocacy.org or (515) 555-0146.
