Northfold Quarterly is an independent magazine covering regional food and agriculture in the Upper Midwest, published from an editorial office at 212 Water Street in Duluth, Minnesota. Readers arrive from a shared link or a search and need a headline-driven homepage that surfaces the newest long-form features alongside a few evergreen guides, since most first-time visitors read one article and leave unless something else immediately catches their eye. Each article page should show the author's byline and short bio, related stories at the bottom, and a clear but unobtrusive subscribe prompt after the second paragraph. The magazine publishes four issues a year in print and posts new stories online weekly, covering farmers, chefs, and the policy fights that shape the regional food system. A free newsletter recaps the week's stories every Friday. The editorial office is reachable Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at editors@northfoldquarterly.com or (218) 555-0156. Northfold Quarterly is published by a registered Minnesota nonprofit media organization. The primary call to action across the site is subscribing to the newsletter or the print edition.
