Solace Health Foundation's development office, based at 415 Grove Street in Hartford, Connecticut, runs major-gift fundraising for a regional hospital system with a team of five gift officers. Every morning the director wants four numbers up top: dollars raised this month, major-gift conversations currently open, average gift size, and year-over-year donor retention. Beneath that, a pipeline table should list one row per prospect — the donor's last name, the gift officer who owns the relationship, the stage from identified through cultivating, solicited, and closed, the ask amount, and a plain-language next step such as a scheduled lunch meeting. An activity feed reads like short field notes: a note logged after a call, a stage change, a stewardship letter sent, most recent entries first. One contact card should surface the largest open ask the team is currently cultivating, and a chart should show dollars raised per week over the last eight weeks. The development office is staffed Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at development@solacehealthfoundation.org or (860) 555-0182. Solace Health Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Connecticut. Filters on the dashboard should cover officer and stage.
