Harbourline Homes is a directory of homes to rent and buy along the Cornish coast, listed by local
agents. We want a one-page site where a visitor searches many listings and books a viewing on the
one they like.

Headline: "Find a place by the sea, from people who know the street." Every listing comes from a
local agent who has walked through the door. Search by town, then filter by bedrooms, budget and
how far you want to be from the water. Search field placeholder: "Search by town or postcode".
Button: "Search homes".

Nav: Rent, Buy, Holiday lets, Agents, with a "List a property" call to action.

Browse by kind of home: Harbour cottages (stone cottages a short walk from the quay), Family
houses (three bedrooms and up, with gardens and parking), Flats with a view (upper-floor flats
that look out over the bay), Holiday lets (furnished homes for a week or a season).

Filters: bedrooms (one, two, three or more), budget (under 1,200 a month, 1,200 to 1,800 a month,
over 1,800 a month), distance to sea (under 5 minutes, under 15 minutes, any distance). Results
can sort by newest, lowest price, or nearest the sea.
