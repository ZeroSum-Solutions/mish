Kiln Row Collective is a members' studio and community for independent makers and small creative
businesses in Bristol. We want a one-page site that gets a visitor to join.

Hero: "A shared studio and a community that has your back." Kiln Row Collective gives makers,
designers and small creative businesses a working studio, kiln access, and a community that shows
up for each other's launches. 68 members and counting. Button: "Join Kiln Row".

Nav: Studio, Membership, Events, Members, with a "Become a member" call to action.

What members get: 24-hour studio access (badge in any time, day or night, to bench space, kiln
slots and shared tools), kiln and equipment access (two electric kilns, a spray booth and a wood
shop, booked through the members' calendar), a working community (critique nights, skill shares
and a group that actually looks at your work), discounted workshops (half price on every workshop
the studio runs, for you and one guest).

Membership tiers, pick the level of access that matches how often you're in the studio: Studio Day
$45/month (drop-in bench time for occasional use — 8 studio hours a month, shared tool access,
members' chat channel, "Start Studio Day"). Full Bench $120/month (a dedicated bench and regular
kiln slots — unlimited studio hours, one kiln slot a week, locker and shelf space, "Start Full
Bench"). Studio Partner $260/month (for a small creative business based at Kiln Row — two
dedicated benches, priority kiln booking, listed as a studio partner, "Talk to us").

How joining works: visit the studio (come by on a Tuesday open night and see the space before you
commit), pick a tier (Studio Day, Full Bench or a partner bench, based on how often you'll be in),
get your induction (a 30-minute kiln and safety induction, then you're badged in).

Upcoming at the studio: open critique night, first Tuesday monthly (bring current work for a group
look, no RSVP needed). Glaze chemistry workshop, 14 October (a half-day workshop on mixing your
own glazes, led by a member). Winter open studios, last weekend of January (members open their
benches to the public for one weekend).

What members say: "I moved my whole practice here in a month and never looked back." — Priya
Odedra, Ceramicist. "The kiln access alone pays for the membership." — Femi Adeyemi, Product
designer. "Having other makers around changed how seriously I take my own work." — Elin Karlsson,
Illustrator.

Questions before you join: Do I need my own tools? No — shared hand tools and machines are
included; bring your own only if you prefer. Can I pause my membership? Yes, pause for up to two
months a year with two weeks' notice. Is there a joining fee? No joining fee — you pay the first
month's membership and that's it. Can I bring a guest? Members can bring a guest to open nights
and workshops at no extra charge.

Ready to join: tell us what you make and how often you'd use the studio — we reply within two
working days. Button: "Apply for membership". Phone 0117 555 0148, email join@kilnrow.example.
