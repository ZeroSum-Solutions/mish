Willow Creek Family Dental is a family and cosmetic dental practice in Northeast Portland, Oregon.
We've treated NE Portland families since 2009. We want a one-page website for people deciding
whether to book with us.

What we offer: preventive care (cleanings, exams, digital X-rays for every age, twice a year),
restorative dentistry (fillings, crowns and bridges with tooth-colored materials), and cosmetic
dentistry (whitening, bonding, porcelain veneers).

Our providers: Dr. Elena Marsh, DDS (general and cosmetic dentistry, University of Washington
School of Dentistry, practicing since 2008); Dr. Priya Anand, DMD (restorative dentistry, Oregon
Health & Science University, practicing since 2015); Sofia Reyes, RDH (registered dental
hygienist, Pacific University, on staff since 2019).

What a new patient should expect: check-in and a short health history, an exam with digital
X-rays, then a cleaning and a clear plan with costs explained up front before any work starts.

We accept most major PPO plans (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife) — our front desk verifies benefits
before billing. A new-patient cleaning and exam without insurance is $149.

Patients can book online at https://booking.willowcreekdental.example/appointments or call us at
(503) 555-0142.
