Lowmoor Crossing is a small crossroads village up in the hill country. We just want a very simple
one-page site — nothing fancy, just something that gives a visitor a feel for the place before
they decide to come.

Lowmoor Crossing grew up around the ford where the old drovers' road crosses the Lowmoor Beck.
There's a stone bridge now instead of the ford, a single row of shops either side of the crossing,
and a green where the road widens out. Most people who come through are walking the long-distance
Ridgeway path, which passes straight through the village square.

The one other thing worth saying: the green hosts a small farmers' stall most weekends, run by a
couple of the surrounding farms, selling whatever's in season.

We don't have set opening hours to give you, we don't have an address to give beyond "the village
square," we don't have named landmarks beyond what's already said above, and we don't have a
website or booking link to send people to. Please don't put any of that on the page — just the
two things above.
