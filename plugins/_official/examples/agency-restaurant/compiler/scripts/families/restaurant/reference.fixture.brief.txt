Vela Trattoria is a wood-fired Italian kitchen in Seattle's Fremont neighborhood: handmade pasta,
a short natural wine list and a nightly changing oven special. We want a one-page site for
someone deciding whether to book a table.

Nav: Menu, Reservations, Our story, Visit, with a "Reserve a table" call to action.

Headline: handmade pasta and a wood-fired oven, six nights a week. We roll pasta every morning
and keep the oven running until close — stop by the bar for a plate or book ahead for a full
table. Button: "Book a table".

Menu highlights, a short seasonal menu built around what the market and the wood oven give us
that day: cacio e pepe (fresh tonnarelli, Pecorino Romano, cracked black pepper) $19; wood-fired
margherita (San Marzano tomato, fior di latte, basil, olive oil) $18; braised short rib
pappardelle (slow-braised beef, red wine, pappardelle, gremolata) $27; roasted cauliflower
(chili, garlic, lemon, breadcrumb, mint) $14; affogato (vanilla gelato, hot espresso, cocoa nib)
$9.

Hours: Tuesday-Thursday 5-9:30pm, Friday-Saturday 5-10pm, closed Sunday and Monday. We're at 412
Fremont Ave N, Seattle, WA 98103, phone 206-555-0148.

Reservations: we hold half the dining room for walk-ins, but a booking guarantees your table on a
Friday or Saturday night. Button: "Reserve now".

Our story: Chef Marco Belline opened Vela Trattoria in 2019 after a decade cooking in Bologna and
Seattle kitchens. The menu changes with the season, but the wood oven never goes cold.

A look inside, for the gallery: the wood-fired oven (burning apple and oak all night), the pasta
bar (tonnarelli and pappardelle, rolled fresh each morning), and the dining room (twelve tables
and six seats at the bar).

What diners say: "The cacio e pepe alone is worth the wait for a table." — Dana Okafor, "Best
