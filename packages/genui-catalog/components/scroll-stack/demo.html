Getting started

Launch your first project

Step 1 of 42 min

Create a workspace

Name it, pick a region and invite the two or three people you work with most.

Step 2 of 45 min

Connect your data

Link a spreadsheet or database. Nothing is copied until you choose what to sync.

Step 3 of 44 min

Shape the first view

Drag fields into a board, a timeline or a table. Every view stays live and editable.

Step 4 of 41 min

Share and go live

Publish a read-only link or add teammates as editors. You can change access at any time.
