Client onboarding review, Q3 2026

New clients wait nine working days before their first delivered request. This review finds where the days go and proposes a five-day target for Q4.

Summary

We onboarded 14 clients between July and September. The median time from signed contract to first delivered request was nine working days. Most of that time was spent waiting: for access to the client's accounts, and for a kickoff call that fits both calendars.

Recommendation: collect account access in the contract form and offer kickoff slots in the welcome email. Together these remove an estimated four days.

Proposed changes for Q4

  1. Ask for access in the contract form. Add a required "technical contact" field and a checklist of the accounts we need. Owner: Operations.
  2. Offer kickoff slots in the welcome email. Send three times within five working days. The client picks one; no back-and-forth. Owner: Account leads.
  3. Start the workspace set-up before kickoff. Set-up needs the contract only, so it can run in parallel. Owner: Operations.

Risks

  • Some clients cannot name a technical contact at signing. For those, the account lead follows up on day one.
  • A five-day target leaves no slack for public holidays. We will measure in working days and report exceptions.
