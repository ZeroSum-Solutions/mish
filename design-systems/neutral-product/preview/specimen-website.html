How a request moves through Plainfield

The same three steps for a one-line fix or a month-long project.

  1. Step 1

    Capture

    A client emails support@, fills in your portal form, or calls. Plainfield files the request under the right client.

  2. Step 2

    Assign

    A lead picks an owner and a due date. The owner gets one notification, not five.

  3. Step 3

    Close and bill

    The owner marks it done. The client sees it in their portal, and billable time moves to the next invoice.

Questions teams ask before they switch

Can we keep our support email address?

Yes. Forward it to your Plainfield address. Replies still come from your own domain after you verify it.

How do we move our client list?

Upload a CSV with one row per client. Plainfield matches columns by name and shows every row it cannot read before it imports anything.

Do clients need an account?

No. Clients can reply by email. The portal is optional and uses a sign-in link, not a password.

What happens to our data if we leave?

Export everything as CSV and PDF at any time. After you cancel, data stays available for 30 days and is then deleted.
