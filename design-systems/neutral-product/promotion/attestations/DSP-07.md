# DSP-07 — touch and keyboard

- Checker: GPT-6 Sol (delegated by Devin)
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Verdict: **fail**
- Method: Temporary Playwright Node script under `e2e/`; opened each of four specimens and `components.html` by `file://` at 1280, 768, and 360px. Tabbed through the full closed-state page, checked focus rectangles against the viewport and `elementFromPoint`, pressed Enter on links and buttons, Space on buttons and choice controls, ArrowDown on selects and menus, ArrowRight on tabs and radios, and Escape on open navigation. Measured visible interactive rectangles against 24px. The script was deleted after the check.
- Environment: macOS arm64, Node 24.16.0, **headless Chromium 148.0.7778.96**, desktop emulation at 1280/768/360 × 900. No physical touch device.

## Findings

- The website menu, app navigation, and component shell navigation opened by keyboard and closed with Escape. Native checkboxes and radios changed state. Website FAQ disclosures toggled. Focus order for every page and width is recorded below.
- In the open app navigation at 768 and 360px, Tab from `Settings` reaches `#nav-toggle` while the sidebar covers its center. `elementFromPoint` did not return the toggle or a child. The same failure occurs for `#pattern-nav-toggle` in `components.html` at 768 and 360px. See `preview/specimen-app.html:348`, `components.html:665`, and the covered-focus captures in `evidence/`.
- The app's four `[role="tab"]` controls at `preview/specimen-app.html:360-364` did not change selection on Enter or ArrowRight. The component fixture's tabs at `components.html:601` behaved the same way. Its `[role="menu"]` at `components.html:618-623` did not move focus with ArrowDown. Both switches at `components.html:542-543` kept `aria-checked` unchanged after Enter and Space. Many specimen buttons are static examples, as `components.html:488` states, but they remain exposed as controls.
- The two `.switch` buttons measured **36 × 20px** (`components.html:269-273`), below the 24px height floor. The toast `Dismiss` button measured **18 × 32px** at 360px (`components.html:603`). Several text links have boxes under 24px high; inline-text exceptions may apply. Native 16 × 16px checkboxes have associated labels, so I did not count their input rectangle alone as a conclusive target failure.
- Closed-state focus was fully unobscured except the partially clipped `Archived` tab in the 360px app and the `Files` tab in the 360px component fixture. The covered toggle in the open state is the decisive focus failure.
